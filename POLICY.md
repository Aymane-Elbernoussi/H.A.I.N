# Hotel Policy Spec (v0.1)

This is the product spec Prelint enforces. Every agent-proposed guest reply
and every comp/refund decision is checked against these rules before it ships.

## Guest communication

1. Replies must be professional, empathetic, and specific to the guest's issue.
   No generic templates that ignore what the guest actually said.
2. Never promise compensation amounts in a first reply. Acknowledge the issue,
   state the action being taken, and say a manager will follow up on
   compensation where appropriate.
3. Health/safety issues (pests, mold, alarms, lock failures) are always
   critical urgency. Acknowledge within the reply and escalate to a human
   immediately — do not wait for the next batch.
4. Never disclose internal operations, staffing, or system details.

## Compensation & refunds

5. Comps up to $50: agent may propose, Policy gate approves.
6. Comps $50–$200: require human manager approval in the BAND room.
7. Comps above $200 or any cash refund: require human approval AND a ledger
   entry with a reason before the reply goes out.
8. Every comp/refund must attach to a case with a category and reason.
   No orphan ledger entries.

## Tone

9. Match the hotel's voice: warm, direct, no corporate jargon, no emojis
   in guest-facing replies.
